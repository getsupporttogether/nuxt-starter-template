**The Most Important Element of Effective Psychotherapy is the Genuine Connection Between Client and Therapis**t

My approach to therapy is interactive, supportive and empathic. I see psychotherapy as a creative process, with you and I as partners in this highly individualized process of exploration and discovery.

I've found that the most effective therapy is focused collaboration between you and me. We'll work together exploring feelings of depression, anxiety, or shame which are often the result of trauma.

My specialty integrating Eye Movement Desensitization Reprocessing (EMDR), Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), and insight-oriented techniques to best address your areas of growth potential. Psychotherapy helps people explore new ways of thinking and behaving – the building blocks for improvement.

## Let's Start Your Therapeutic Journey

Together we're going to roll up our sleeves and work at your pace as your mind, body, and lifestyle will allow you to on your quest for emotional regulation and balance.

I have specialized training in helping people heal from traumas, concerns in the present, or fears and worries about their future.  For many, these are considered the stuck points or triggers, which take control, rather than being the master of your own destiny.

Frequently, people come to therapy looking to make a significant change in their lives, or hopes of convincing those in their lives to make changes for them.  We'll approach those things in your control, how to build (or re-build) trust, confidence, and hope!
