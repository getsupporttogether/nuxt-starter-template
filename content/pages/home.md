Working you with in partnership is how we accomplish solutions specific to your unique needs. I strive to create an atmosphere of support and nurturance, and my clients often tell me they feel truly listened to and understood.

My style as a therapist is characterized as warm and inviting. My purpose is to collaborate with you to transform your depression, anxiety, or anger into inside, change, and hope. I respect your natural intuitions towards healing, and together we'll explore ways to break through past barriers to bring increased balance and wholeness to your life.
