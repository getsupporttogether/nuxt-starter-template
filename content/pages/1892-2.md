## PRIVACY POLICY   Leslie Tourish, Licensed Professional Counselor

## Privacy Policy

Leslie Tourish, Licensed Professional Counselor, Dripping Springs, Texas Privacy Policy

Last Updated on October 24, 2026. This privacy policy is effective immediately and replaces any previously issued Privacy Policies.

The respect of your privacy and my commitment to protecting it is a cornerstone of a strong patient/therapist bond, and I take it very seriously.  Information may be collected based on your data entry into website forms, through text or other electronic messages from this website, and e-mail.  The following policy details what information may be collected from you and my dedication to safely collecting, using, maintaining, and protecting your personal information.

**THERAPEUTIC TERMS & CONDITIONS**

When approaching therapy, it is your time to use as you see fit, and I’m a guide to assist in using the time wisely.  Refunds are not issued from sessions and my cancellation policy requires any cancellations be made at least 24 hours in advance.

When you submit a website form, I collect the contact details, request type and message you provide to respond to your inquiry. Netlify processes and stores these submissions and sends their contents to me by email. Blog-update requests are handled manually and do not automatically enroll you in a mailing list.

Please do not include sensitive health information, medical records, payment details or urgent requests. These forms are not a secure clinical messaging service and should not be used for emergencies.

**CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION**

All information stored by the Licensed Professional Counseling practice of Leslie Tourish is treated as confidential.  All such data is accessed only by authorized personnel and security is in place to protect against breach or loss.  Information includes, but is not limited to, information provided directly to me through intake or other forms, submission of information through the [Contact](</contact/>) page, and any other methods.

Credit card details processed by Leslie Tourish are not stored on any host servers and must be provided as a payment method each time of use.

**AFFILIATE LINKS**

At times I may use affiliate links to support reviews and resources from authors, speakers, podcasters, and other influential mental health providers.  Links may be seen on my [Blog](</blog/>) or Resource pages.

**ACCEPTANCE OF CONDITIONS**

I have the right to assume all site visitors and potential clients have carefully read this notice.  If you do not agree with this policy, please refrain from using the website or submitting online form requests.

This privacy policy may be updated, without notice, at any time as necessary, and version history will be available.

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, then please contact me at leslie@leslietourish.com

**CONTACTING ME**

I welcome your questions or comments regarding this Privacy Policy. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or would like further information, please contact me Leslie Tourish, 4207 East U.S. Highway 290, Suite 111, Dripping Springs, TX 78620 or leslie@leslietourish.com.
