## Mandalas Symbolize the Transformation from Suffering to Joy

My primary goal in working with clients is to help them find personal well-being and balance.  When entering therapy, one may be unbalanced, traumatized, or hopeless.  Our journey together will be one towards emotional strength and joy.

## I'm Leslie Tourish.   It's Nice to Meet You.

As a person who has transformed her own life, it's a privilege to be a guide for men, women, and couples looking to evolve the way they think and behave to lead a happier life.

After earning a bachelor's degree in journalism, I worked for 13 years as a photojournalist for newspapers.  A desire to get out from behind the camera and build deeper relationships with people brought me to a fulfilling career in psychotherapy.

With a master's degree in human services from St. Edward’s University, I launched into learning from a variety of settings, including an MHMR clinic, a pre-release prison focusing on chemical dependency issues, a psychiatric hospital, and an adoption agency. Since 2002, I've been in private practice in Dripping Springs working with adolescents, adults, and couples.

## **Issues I Work With**

![](<https://leslietourish.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/leslie_tourish_psychotherapist_dripping_springs_texas_depression_treatment_therapy-2.png?w=1024>)

**DEPRESSION**

Everyone gets the blues from time to time. But depression takes a person down deeper, stretches out longer, and if unchecked, can be considered a medical condition. Gain knowledge about ways to manage depression.

![](<https://leslietourish.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/leslie_tourish_psychotherapist_dripping_springs_texas_anxiety_treatment_therapy.png?w=1024>)

**ANXIETY**

Our world can be an anxiety-producing place. However, a person suffering from an anxiety disorder may be under a persistent cloud of restlessness, sometimes resulting in panic attacks. Due to racing thoughts, they may find it difficult to focus and follow through in their daily lives.

![](<https://leslietourish.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/leslie_tourish_psychotherapist_dripping_springs_texas_trauma_treatment_therapy.jpg?w=1024>)

**TRAUMA**

When someone experiences a terrible event, their immediate response may be to shut down with shock and denial. If the trauma isn’t processed, their emotional state may shift into mood swings, flashbacks, tearfulness, and extreme agitation, leaving them unable to move on in their lives.

![](<https://leslietourish.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/leslie_tourish_psychotherapist_dripping_springs_texas_helplessness_treatment_therapy.png?w=1024>)

**FEELINGS OF HELPLESSNESS**

If someone is in a state of helplessness, they may feel weakened, dependent, and struggling to handle their daily adult activities. This lack of agency may increase their belief that they are powerless to make the changes to improve their life, thus reinforcing their helplessness.

![](<https://leslietourish.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/leslie_tourish_psychotherapist_dripping_springs_texas_hopelessness_treatment_therapy.jpg?w=1024>)

**FEELINGS OF HOPELESSNESS**

Hopelessness is a bleak emotion. Someone may feel that options are limited, if non-existent, leaving them with a deepening sense of desperation.

![](<https://leslietourish.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/leslie_tourish_psychotherapist_dripping_springs_texas_shame_treatment_therapy.png?w=1024>)

**SHAME**

This is a painful emotion in which a person believes they are inherently flawed, thus unworthy as a human being. If this belief is unchecked, a person could shut down and avoid growth opportunities thus keeping them more firmly into the self-sabotaging shame cycle.

## Professional Experience

After pursuing a career in photojournalism, I embarked on a journey of getting my Licensed Professional Counseling Degree. I benefitted from powerful training and learning experiences on the therapeutic process before entering private practice. During my earlier years, I worked in residential treatment centers, drug/rehab facilities, an adoption agency, St. David's Hospital Psychiatric Unit, and more.

Working with clients in private practice has been my favorite way to make the biggest impact in people's lives.
